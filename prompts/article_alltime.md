## Required Article Structure (HTML format)

1. <h1> — engaging title for the all-time overview
2. <p> Lead — 2-3 sentence summary
3. <h2> Growth Story — how the project grew over the years, show dynamics as <table> (year, observations, species, new species, observers)
4. <h2> Species Discovery — cumulative species curve description, new species per year, discovery rate trends
5. <h2> Taxonomic Overview — top families and species as <table>, diversity highlights
6. <h2> Community — top contributors as <table>, how participation evolved
7. <h2> Geographic Patterns — if district data available, show as <table>
8. <h2> Limitations — MUST include:
   - iNaturalist data reflect observer activity, not a complete biodiversity census
   - Lack of observations does not imply absence
   - Year-to-year changes may reflect effort, not ecology
9. <h2> Conclusion — project trajectory, outlook

Write in clean HTML. Use tables for data. Cite numbers from the provided data. Keep it engaging but scientifically responsible.
